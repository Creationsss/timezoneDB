Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2025

What Timezone DB collects when you use it, where that data lives, and how to remove it.

Key points

  • We keep your Discord user ID, username and the timezone you choose.
  • Your username and timezone are public through the API. That is what the service is for.
  • You can delete your timezone at any time, and sessions expire after 1 hour.
  • We don't sell your data or use cookies for tracking.

What we collect

When you use Timezone DB, we collect only what the service needs to work:

Data Where it comes from Kept for
Discord user ID and username Discord OAuth Until you delete your timezone
Discord discriminator and avatar Discord OAuth 1 hour, inside your session
Timezone You choose it Until you delete it
Session token Created when you log in 1 hour

How we use your data

We use your information only to:

  • Store and display your timezone preference
  • Authenticate you through Discord OAuth
  • Provide API access to your stored timezone data
  • Display basic statistics (user counts, popular timezones)

Data storage

Your user data is stored in our PostgreSQL database. Sessions are held in Redis and expire after 1 hour. We don't use cookies for tracking or analytics.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your username and timezone are public through our API endpoints, because publishing them is what the service does.

Data retention

Your data is stored until you delete it with the "Delete Timezone" button or by contacting us. Session data expires automatically after 1 hour.

Your rights

You can:

  • Update your timezone at any time
  • Delete your timezone data using the delete button
  • Log out to end your session immediately

Third-party services

We use Discord OAuth for authentication. See Discord's Privacy Policy for how Discord handles your data.

Security

We protect your data with secure session management and database access controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us through our project page.
