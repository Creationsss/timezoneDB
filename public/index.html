How it works

Set it once

Your timezone stays until you change or delete it.

  1. Log in with Discord

    We only request the identify scope, so we see your public profile and not your servers or messages.

  2. Pick your timezone

    Search every IANA timezone, including older names like America/Montreal, or use the one your browser reports.

  3. Your time shows up

    Anyone using a supported plugin sees your local time. The integrations page lists them.
